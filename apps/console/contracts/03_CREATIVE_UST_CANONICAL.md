# CREATIVE UST — CANONICAL SUNO LYRICS-PROMPT OUTPUT FORMAT

## Identity
Creative UST is the definitive Suno lyrics-prompt output format.
It is derivative and downstream.
It is not the deep worksheet.
It is not the canonical truth object.
It is the compressed expressive shell projected from the deeper Technical UST.

## Lyric row law
"lyrics line", (adlib) ** sfx: OPTIONAL CUE **

Rules:
- lyric text stays on the lyric row
- adlib stays on the lyric row
- local sfx cue stays on the lyric row
- section header stands alone on its own line
- blank lines appear between lyric rows only when useful for breath-line readability

## Forbidden legacy constructs
- [CREW_TAGS]
- [Road-Map]
- standalone FX block
- [Lead:] or [Choir:] labels inside quoted lyrics
- parenthetical stage direction inside lyric quotes
- uncontained note-data outside the canonical blocks

## Derivation law
Creative UST must preserve the highest-leverage creative information from the Technical UST while remaining safe for Suno-facing use.
It may compress.
It may not flatten meaning for convenience.
Character savings are fuel for preserved nuance, not the goal themselves.
